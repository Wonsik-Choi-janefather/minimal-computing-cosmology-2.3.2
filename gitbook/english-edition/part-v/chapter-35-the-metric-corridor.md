# Chapter 35 The Metric Corridor

Even if recoverable states exist, a collapse of the localization metric prevents the system from being said to maintain the same space. The benchmark directly places a common reference metric g\_ref in the localization factor and sets g\_N = g\_ref for every N.

$$
c_N g_ref ≤ g_N ≤ C_N g_ref
$$

_Equation (5.13)_

Under this choice, the uniform ellipticity constants are c\_N = C\_N = 1, and the condition number is also one. Metric rank and directional length scales are therefore maintained exactly as the order increases.

$$
g_N=g_ref, c_N=C_N=1, cond(g_N)=1
$$

_Equation (5.14)_

This result is an exact statement by construction, not a numerical discovery. The metric corridor is automatically preserved because it is a tensor factor independent of the spectral approximation.

At the same time, the reason this does not explain the origin of three-dimensional space is clear: the reference metric and dimension were inputs. The benchmark shows that a given space can be preserved, but does not calculate how three directions are realized from LAW's dimensional structure.

A stronger model must make g\_N a function of the spectral state and recovery current rather than holding it fixed. It must then test whether uniform ellipticity and a common characteristic cone survive under actual dynamics.

The present assessment is metric preservation PASS, dynamical geometry origin OPEN. Both statements must be recorded together to prevent input space from being mistaken for predicted space.
