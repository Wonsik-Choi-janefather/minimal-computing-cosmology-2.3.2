# Chapter 29 From Declaration to Computation

Thus far, WRRA has organized the conditions required to realize and recover reality as a declarative interface. But the labels LAW, SOURCE, RENDERER, and PHENOTYPE do not by themselves perform an actual calculation. At minimum, one Hilbert space, a self-adjoint Hamiltonian, an observation port, and a recovery channel must be constructed in finite form.

The goal of Part V is not to replicate the entire universe inside a computer. It constructs a minimal existence benchmark that approximates a positive continuous spectrum with a finite Jacobi matrix, calculates a Euclidean resolvent and real-time survival amplitude upon it, and attaches recovery dynamics in separate localization and syndrome layers.

$$
H_N = H_Jacobi ⊗ H_loc ⊗ H_syn
$$

_Equation (5.1)_

This benchmark is factorized. Spectral simulation, the localization metric, and the recovery channel are placed in different tensor factors. Each condition can therefore be passed explicitly, but the work does not claim that all three elements emerged spontaneously from one microscopic interaction.

The central computational paper is Finite-Jacobi Compatibility Benchmark for WRRA v1.2, released on August 27, 2026. The open Version 1.2 received DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22126918 and supplements the time-uniform WRRA paper, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22126125, with a numerical benchmark.

The criteria for computational success are also restricted. Positive weights, real nodes, a self-adjoint finite Hamiltonian, a rank-one visible port, convergence on a finite Euclidean window, an explicit recovery gap, and bounded syndrome entropy must coexist.

$$
Benchmark = spectral simulation + metric corridor + recovery ledger
$$

_Equation (5.2)_

The meaning of this construction is not that “the universe has been proven to be a simulation.” It is the first constructive evidence that some declarative conditions of WRRA are mutually consistent and can be realized simultaneously within one finite computational ledger.
