## An Open Future Requires a Separate Condition

The fact that Reality reveals only part of the Actual does not imply that the future is ontologically open. Behind the same noninjective renderer there may be a deterministic hidden completion in which every outcome is already fixed. Rendering deficit and event openness are distinct claims.

WRRA v1.3 separates them through the No Predetermined Event Register condition. LAW owns the state space, evolution of complex amplitudes, correlation structure, conservation laws, boundary conditions, and allowed transition algebra; SOURCE is η acting once on that structure. Neither LAW nor SOURCE may contain a classical register from which the future event sequence (a₁,a₂,…,a∞) can be read directly. This condition is a POSTULATE requiring tests, not a derived theorem.

The quantum instrument supplies normalized probabilities over allowed outcomes and conditional updates, then appends only one of them to the single history. This interface does not require many worlds, but neither does it mathematically refute them. WRRA adopts the single-history boundary: “Possibilities may be multiple, but the physical event record is one.”

The present cosmological view can therefore be summarized as law-closed, render-open, and event-open.
