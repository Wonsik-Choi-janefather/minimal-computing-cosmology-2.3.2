Conclusion of this chapter: Entropy is not time itself or a WRRA activity coordinate. It is the probability-weighted multiplicity of microscopic states and directional histories that can no longer be distinguished or reconstructed from the present macrostate under a specified observer, coarse-graining, and resolution.

Directional history h\_X measures past bias between return and outgoing fluxes, but is not entropy itself.
