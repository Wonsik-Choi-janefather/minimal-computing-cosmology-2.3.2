# Chapter 52 The Physical Realization of Superheavy-Nucleus Stability and Half-Life

Conclusion of this chapter: A restricted alpha-partial-half-life benchmark supplied with evaluated Q\_α passed, but the calculation that generates masses, Q values, and all decay widths from Z and N alone to predict total half-life and the dominant channel has not yet closed.

The alpha-decay baseline froze Royer-type coefficients log₁₀(T\_α/s) = a+bA^(1/6)√Z+cZ/√Q\_α+h\_odd on 254 exact-mass alpha emitters with Z = 84–103 and evaluated the holdout Z ≥ 104. The MAE was 0.606 decades for all 84 cases and 0.438 decades for the thirteen with exact masses and half-lives. A published modified Viola–Seaborg equation performed better on the same full sample, at 0.565 decades, so the result is not assigned as a WRRA-specific prediction.

log10(Tα/s)=a+bA^(1/6)√Z+cZ/√Qα+hodd (8.15)

The five-term liquid-drop-mass route Z,N → Q\_α produced a Q\_α MAE of 0.398 MeV and a consequent T\_α MAE of 1.210 decades. Because superheavy-nucleus half-lives depend exponentially on Q\_α, even an error of several hundred keV is greatly amplified. This is a failure of that liquid-drop baseline, not of every microscopic–macroscopic or EDF/HFB mass model.

For spontaneous fission, broad extrapolation of the published Xu–Ren equation to 43 median-value cases with Z ≥ 104 yielded an MAE of 5.341 decades. Its departure from the original equation's domain near the long-lived line N = Z+52 is recorded; the result is not generalized into a failure of every empirical SF model.

Predictive closure requires calculating constrained-HFB masses, deformations, and pairing in the same EDF and basis, together with barrier penetrability P\_ℓ and microscopic formation amplitude F\_α for the alpha channel, multidimensional least-action paths, collective inertia, and zero-point energy for fission, and weak-decay and other competing widths. Only after calculating Γ\_tot = Σ\_c Γ\_c can one claim T\_1/2 = ℏ ln 2/Γ\_tot.

Γα=2Pℓ(Qα,R)γα², Γtot=ΣcΓc, T1/2=ℏ ln2/Γtot (8.16)

Assessment: the evaluated-Q\_α alpha systematics are a RESTRICTED EMPIRICAL BENCHMARK PASS; the exact-data subset n = 13 requires further validation; the five-term liquid-drop Q\_α route and broad Xu–Ren SF extrapolation are REJECTED FOR THIS SCOPE; and HFB hybrid total-half-life execution and any WRRA residual effect are OPEN/NOT YET RUN.
