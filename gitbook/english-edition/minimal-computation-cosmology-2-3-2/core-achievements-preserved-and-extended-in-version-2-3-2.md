**6. A reality sustained by matter alone.** The framework separates LAW's permission of an antimatter sector from the persistence of long-occupied Reality in a matter-dominant or matter-only state. Symmetry in the space of possibilities and asymmetry in actual occupancy are not contradictory.

**9. The ability to read many fields with one tool.** Without changing WRRA Core 1.0, which was developed from cosmology, the framework applies it to life, cells, embodied intelligence, consciousness, economics, meteorology, and power grids, comparing the state, boundary, residue, resources, and failure conditions of each field in the same format.

**10. A research grammar that compresses success and failure together.** WRRA does not collect only positive results. It records, in the same ledger, cases in which a strong baseline wins, a preregistered threshold is missed, data fail to identify the claim, and the conditions for re-entry. A central strength of a general-purpose methodology is that the Domain Profile's judgment can be updated without changing the Core to fit the result.
