# Opening This Book

**From Cosmology to the Language of Complex Systems**

This research began with a single question. Must the universe store in advance the detailed information of every event and particle before it becomes reality? Or does it compose the present from only the states and relations required, then generate the next reality from that present?

Minimal Computation Cosmology explores the second possibility. On this view, the universe is not a device that plays back a completed future. It is an execution system that continuously forms one reality through fixed laws, present states, relations among states, boundary conditions, and records of actual events.

WRRA emerged while this structure was being organized. WRRA separates a complex object into SOURCE, RELATION, BOUNDARY, RESIDUE, UPDATE, and LEDGER. SOURCE is an independent state or input; RELATION is a coupling or transport rule; BOUNDARY is the range of change and observation. RESIDUE is history that remains in the present and affects the next change, UPDATE is the rule for moving to the next present, and LEDGER records resources that are conserved or transferred.

**Minimal Computation → WRRA Core → Domain-Specific Execution Models**

At first, this structure seemed to be a language for explaining the universe. A different possibility emerged, however, when WRRA was applied to cellular execution, gene expression, the origin of life, the sensorimotor circuit of C. elegans, economic shocks, trust and credit cycles, and tropical-cyclone tracks. These objects differ completely in scale and components, yet they can be compared through the common structure of independent states and relation networks, boundary conditions, history remaining in the present, and resource transfers.

WRRA may therefore be not a particular physical model applicable only to the universe, but a general interpretive framework for analyzing information ownership and closure conditions across complex systems. Its direct output is not a single value for every future. It is a structural judgment about what can be reconstructed from the present, where prediction opens, and what form of answer is justified. Minimal Computation Cosmology can be understood anew as the largest and most fundamental case in which this framework is applied to the universe as a whole.

The fact that WRRA works in one field does not, however, automatically prove its truth in another field or in cosmology. Internal success in a cellular model is not a complete account of life, nor is the executability of a neural circuit a reconstruction of the whole of C. elegans. Each application must be tested separately against independent data and experiments.

This principle was tested directly in WRRA-Cell 0.1–0.4. In a synthetic residue-generating system, a model including residue showed a conditional long-horizon predictive gain; in an audit of real static K562 Perturb-seq data, however, the frozen toy relation matrix was not supported. This book preserves that nonconfirmation instead of collecting successes alone. The value of a general framework lies not in declaring that it is correct everywhere, but in specifying which claims each dataset permits or blocks.

WRRA-Econ 0.1–0.5 tested the same principle on more difficult social data. In the synthetic economy, the residue planted as the true cause was recovered again under new seeds, but no universal predictive gain was established in monthly U.S. macroeconomic data. A directional signal after crises remained, yet present consumer sentiment did not consistently beat the 36-month-lag placebo. The economic application is therefore not recorded as a success case but as a frozen study in which the measurement target and temporal alignment remain open.

WRRA-Met 0.1–0.5 prospectively tested whether present residue constructed solely from the track and intensity history of a tropical-cyclone center provides independent information about its future track. A weak directional signal recurred in a restricted linear model, but it had no unique advantage over raw history and failed to outperform a nonlinear current-state baseline at all five forecast horizons. This failure establishes not that “there is no residue,” but the boundary that “the tropical-cyclone center track is not the owner of that residue.” The final judgment is TRACK-ONLY RESIDUE NOT ESTABLISHED / ENVIRONMENTAL STATE REQUIRED.

DOIs 10.5281/zenodo.22330256 and 10.5281/zenodo.22331940 organize these experiences into one methodology. Interpretation applies when currently observed information closes the target to one value; prediction begins when, under the same information, the conditional distribution does not collapse to a single value. This boundary is determined not by macro versus micro scale, equation order, or the distinction between rational and irrational numbers, but by informational closure. WRRA is not a universal predictor; it is an interpreter that reads this boundary.

## **Domains of Possible Extension**

WRRA's range of application is not limited to the universe and life. In principle, the same structure can be tested in any complex system for which independent states, relation networks, boundary conditions, history, and resource transfers can be distinguished.

In meteorological systems, local atmospheric and oceanic states, the transport of heat, moisture, and momentum, and topographic, coastal, and observational boundaries can be separated. In ecosystems, species and populations, food webs, habitats, and energy flows can be mapped to the same roles. In nervous systems and artificial intelligence, neural or computational states, connection networks, input-output boundaries, and the residual effects of memory can be analyzed.

The framework can also be applied to systems in which many components interact to produce collective behavior, including transportation networks, power grids, financial markets, supply chains, epidemics, and phase transitions in materials. The SOURCE and RELATION of each field, however, must be constructed from that field's observational data and established laws rather than assigned arbitrarily by WRRA.

**Does the Same Minimal Structure Recur Across Different Complex Systems?**

The central question ahead is not whether WRRA can be applied everywhere, but whether the same minimal structure is repeatedly recovered across different complex systems. If this recurrence is confirmed through independent data and predictive tests, WRRA can develop beyond a specific cosmological model into a common computational language for comparing and analyzing complex systems.

## **Two Layers on Which to Read This Book**

This book can be read on two layers. The first is Minimal Computation Cosmology, which seeks to explain the beginning of our universe, matter, spacetime, quantum events, and one actual history. The second is a record of testing whether the structural language discovered in that research—WRRA—can also apply to complex systems outside physics.

If the common structure of minimal computation is independently recovered in complex systems as different as the universe, life, economics, and meteorology, minimal computation may be not merely an analytical technique we chose but a universal organizing principle through which complex realities persist. This possibility does not mean reducing every field to one predictive equation. WRRA distinguishes finite-information reconstruction, infinite-structure closure, micro–macro amplification, and information-opening transitions, then first determines which form of answer—a value, proof, finite-time trajectory, or conditional distribution—is justified for each problem.
