# Chapter 26 Falsification Conditions

Falsifiability does not mean only one experiment capable of collapsing an entire theory at once. Because WRRA is layered into architecture, implementation chassis, and the numerical realization of our universe, failures must be assigned to their exact owner.

If two renderer paths yield different gauge-invariant results on the protected observable algebra and no recovery can reconcile them, the path-equivalence implementation fails. If error accumulates without bound under repetition or localization rank collapses, the time-uniform renderer candidate also fails.

$$
sup_n ‖E_n‖ = ∞ ⇒ time-uniform candidate FAIL
$$

_Equation (4.11)_

If bosons and fermions exhibit different characteristic cones that cannot be removed by calibration, the condition of a common spacetime phenotype fails. If recoveries on overlapping regions give contradictory values on the intersection, quasi-local gluing also fails.

In the particle sector, negative spectral density, violation of a Ward identity, a gauge anomaly, an incorrect pole or threshold structure, or independent disagreement with flavor data closes that implementation. A similar curve over a finite window or a positive correlation cannot conceal such failures.

In the cosmological sector, recollapse after opening, failure of scale invariance, excessive backreaction, or an expansion history incompatible with observation is a falsifier. A version fitted by re-entering observational values, however, is a new calibration model rather than a success of the original prediction.

$$
O_T ∉ I_pred ⇒ frozen model FAIL
$$

_Equation (4.12)_

The most important fail-closed rule is silence. If a required calculation is undefined or numerically unstable, neither “correct” nor “incorrect” is declared. OPEN is not a euphemism for success but an independent status meaning that no judgment can yet be made.
