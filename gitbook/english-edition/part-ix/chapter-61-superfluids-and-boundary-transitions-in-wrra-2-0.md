## 61.1 Distinguishing Mercury from Helium

The subject of DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22289094 is not mercury but liquid helium-4 (helium II) below the lambda point T\_λ ≈ 2.17 K. Mercury is a solid superconductor exhibiting loss of electrical resistance and the Meissner response below approximately 4.2 K; the Rollin film climbing over a vessel wall is a mass-transport phenomenon of neutral helium atoms. The two systems share only the mathematical analogy of phase stiffness in a complex order parameter; their transported quantities, gauge couplings, and boundary conditions differ. Neither involves gravitational screening.

## 61.2 Phase Relation and the Two Fluids

The low-energy phase relation can be represented by order parameter Ψ. In strongly interacting liquid helium-4, condensate density n₀ and superfluid mass density ρ\_s are not equal and must be separated. At finite temperature, the superfluid component coexists with a normal component that carries thermal excitations, viscosity, and entropy.

Ψ(r,t)=√n₀(r,t) exp\[iφ(r,t)]

vₛ=(ℏ/m₄)∇φ, jₛ=ρₛvₛ

ρ=ρₛ+ρₙ, j=ρₛvₛ+ρₙvₙ

∮ vₛ·dl = n h/m₄, n = 0, ±1, ±2, …

The circulation quantization and winding conservation in the final equation correspond to a persistent current in a closed loop. Rollin-film outflow, by contrast, is open-system potential flow connecting a reservoir to an external sink and can occur without nontrivial winding. The two phenomena are not combined under the same label “persistent flow.”

## 61.4 From BOUNDARY to CARRY PATH

In the WRRA correspondence, SOURCE is the conserved helium atom number and initial thermodynamic state; RELATION, condensate phase and density; BOUNDARY, the solid–helium interface and free surface; CARRY, mass current in the film; TAKEOVER, the normal component, vortices, heat, and evaporation; and RESET, a change of winding sector or loss of superfluidity. For the boundary to open as a transport path, wetting W, superfluidity with finite ρ\_s S, film connectivity C, a driving force D, and suppression of strong relaxation R must all hold simultaneously.

BOUNDARY→CARRY PATH iff W·S·C·D·R=1
