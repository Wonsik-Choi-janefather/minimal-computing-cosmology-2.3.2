# Chapter 55 Prime SOURCEs and Composite Relation Ports

**The Highest-Level Distinction in Version 2.0**

WRRA 2.0 treats every prime as a mutually independent SOURCE address. This proposition does not mean that each prime has the same physical energy or owned quantity, but is a model axiom that each is an independent label not reducible to a product of smaller natural numbers.

A composite is not a new independent SOURCE but a potential relation address preserving which primes participate with which exponents. Write the prime factorization and incidence of n as follows.

_n = ∏ₚ p^νₚ(n), Bₚₙ = 1\[νₚ(n) > 0], Vₚₙ = νₚ(n)_

An actually open relation is not determined by its address alone. Introduce activation indicator χₙ(k) at present stage k, and separate binary active incidence Bₚₙᵃᶜᵗ(k) = χₙ(k)Bₚₙ from weighted active valuation Vₚₙᵃᶜᵗ(k) = χₙ(k)Vₚₙ. This distinction prevents arithmetic possibility, relation multiplicity, and operation in present Reality from being conflated.

**Chapter Conclusion**

Primes are independent SOURCE labels, composites are relation addresses, and χₙ(k) is actual opening. Separating the three layers avoids the error of directly identifying number-theoretic patterns with particles, charges, or masses.

For compatibility with the minimal-computation principle, the complete set of primes is not stored as a data list. LAW owns the prime-testing and generation rule, while only active ports with nonzero ownership in each fixed present have finite support.
