# Chapter 56 Fixed-Present Ownership and the WJNS Arithmetic Gate

**Fixed-Present State**

The present state is written as a finitely supported nonnegative ownership vector q\_k distributed over prime-indexed ports. q\_k is a dimensionless registry weight, not a physical probability, energy distribution, or quantum density matrix. Q₀ > 0 is the total conserved quantity at this registry layer, not physical energy. Only after normalization p\_k = q\_k/Q₀ is p\_k a coordinate on the probability simplex; q\_k itself lies on a simplex scaled by Q₀.

_q\_k ≥ 0, supp(q\_k) < ∞, 1ᵀq\_k = Q₀_

A normal transition is a positive conservative flow generated by graph Laplacian L, while a reset is represented by column-stochastic matrix R\_k. For a column vector q, the standard sign convention is L\_{ij} ≤ 0 (i ≠ j) and 1ᵀL = 0, and the actual Markov generator is G = −L. Thus G\_{ij} ≥ 0 (i ≠ j) and 1ᵀG = 0.

_dq/dτ\_run = Gq = −Lq, qₖ₊₁ = Rₖ exp(−Δτ\_run Lₖ)qₖ, Gₖ=−Lₖ, 1ᵀRₖ = 1ᵀ_

Here τ\_run is a dimensionless execution parameter. Its correspondence to physical proper time must be determined separately by R\_phys. L\_k is a Laplacian with nonpositive off-diagonal entries and zero column sums, and its generator is G\_k = −L\_k. Positivity and conservation of total quantity under exp(Δτ\_run G\_k) require these sign and zero-sum conditions and an appropriate domain. The rule selecting R\_k and the transfer rule for ownership when a new port opens remain OPEN.

**The WJNS Arithmetic Scale Gate**

In the optional WJNS realization, writing multiplicative scale x as t = log x makes the Mellin transform readable as a Fourier-type phase action. t is a dimensionless arithmetic scale, not physical time. The unitarity condition for phase multiplier U\_ω is U\_ω†U\_ω = I, not generally U\_ω² = I. Only after an explicit reflection J is introduced and W\_ω = JU\_ω defined can the condition W\_ω² = I be tested separately.

Re(s) = 1/2 is used as the structural center of the completed symmetric equation, but this alone is not claimed to prove the Riemann Hypothesis. Global positivity is a separate unresolved condition.

**Chapter Conclusion**

The WJNS gate is a scale-selection device connecting the arithmetic registry to the fixed-present executor. Mathematical exactness, model axioms, and physical interpretation retain separate assessment grades.
