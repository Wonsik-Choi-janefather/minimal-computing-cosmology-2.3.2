## 57.1 Physicalization Interface

Arithmetic labels, Q₀, and the L² norm are not automatically identified with physical energy, quantum probability, or the stress-energy tensor. The lifting E\_lift, which raises the classical registry state q\_k into the quantum Actual, is separated from R\_phys, which connects the Actual to physical fields and observable algebras with units. At present, only requirements have been stated for these maps; they have not yet been derived as completed physical laws.

_prime-port registry → composite valuation registry → \[optional WJNS scale realization] → fixed-present qₖ → transport/reset → carrier/15 channels → \[E\_lift: OPEN] → ρ\_Actual → \[R\_phys: OPEN]/renderer → Reality/Record_

In this chain, the front end performs registration and selection, the middle executes, and the back end performs quantum lifting, physicalization, and representation. E\_lift must define H\_A⊗H\_B or local observable algebras, a positive trace-one ρ\_Actual, and conditions for generating nonseparable states. R\_phys must be derived as an explicit map sending ρ\_Actual to physical objects such as (g\_{μν}, ρ\_QFT, A\_μ, ψ, O\_local, T\_{μν}), or from a covariant action. Until it passes dimensional consistency, Lorentz and gauge/BRST covariance, positivity and normalization, spacelike microcausality, conservation of energy, charge, and probability, and consistency of observer transformations, the connection between the prime registry and observed physics remains OPEN.
