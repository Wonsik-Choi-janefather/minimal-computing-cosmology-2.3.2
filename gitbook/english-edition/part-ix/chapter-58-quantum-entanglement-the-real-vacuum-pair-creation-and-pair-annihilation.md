# Chapter 58 Quantum Entanglement, the Real Vacuum, Pair Creation, and Pair Annihilation

**Quantum Entanglement**

WRRA interprets entanglement not as two objects exchanging a hidden signal but as one nonseparable relation state at the Actual layer producing joint statistics at spatially separated output ports. Nonnegative registry weights q\_k and Laplacian or stochastic reset are, however, classical stochastic processes by themselves and produce neither quantum entanglement nor Bell–CHSH violation. E\_lift must define a composite Hilbert space H\_A⊗H\_B or commuting local algebras, a nonseparable ρ\_AB, local noncommuting observables, and the Born rule, reproducing CHSH correlations and no-signalling together. Until then, entanglement is an ontological interpretation in WRRA, not an independent derivation.

**The Reality Vacuum**

The WRRA reality vacuum is neither a zero vector with no Actual state nor a state in which the amplitudes of all fields are literally zero. The prime registry can be called a set of physical field modes only after passing through E\_lift and R\_phys. It is defined as a zero-real-particle-output phenotype when, for a specified stationary background, observer worldline, detector coupling, and switching prescription, the long-time stationary excitation rate of the stable reference vacuum and the net energy flux across the boundary are zero. ⟨N⟩ = 0 does not mean renormalized ⟨T\_{μν}⟩ = 0 or Wightman correlation G⁺(x,x′) = 0; vacuum correlations, entanglement, and fluctuations at the Actual layer can therefore coexist with stationary zero output at the Reality layer.

**pair creation**

In the reference vacuum of a stable stationary background, the continuing production rate of on-shell particle pairs is zero; internal lines and vacuum polarization in perturbation theory are not literalized as actual pairs appearing and disappearing in time. Nonzero real-particle output or thermal detector response can occur under conditions that alter positive frequency, particle definition, or detector response, including external fields, time-dependent boundaries, collisions, nonstationary geometry, horizons, or accelerated observers. Because a nonstationary spacetime may lack a global energy definition, the ledger must close through work done by the background, local renormalized T\_{μν}, and energy exchange with the detector. WRRA's quantitative rates must first agree with the standard QFT benchmark equations for Schwinger, dynamical-Casimir, and curvature- or horizon-induced particle production.

**pair annihilation**

Pair annihilation is not deletion of existence, but an event in which ownership at particle ports is conservatively transformed into photons or other carrier modes. Immediately after annihilation, emitted modes remain as actual output, so the region does not instantly become vacuum. Only after that output leaves the region or is absorbed into a stationary background can the local renderer again exhibit a zero-output vacuum; the Record is not rewound.

**Chapter Conclusion**

A stationary vacuum is a phenotype of a stationary zero-real-particle production rate for a specified background, observer, and detector prescription; vacuum correlations and renormalized stress-energy belong to separate ledgers. Pair creation is output under a driven process or a changed particle concept caused by altered background, boundary, or observer conditions, while pair annihilation is a conservative transformation leaving emitted modes. This is an ontological reinterpretation. Until it makes a preregistered prediction distinct from standard QFT, it is not claimed as discovery of a new physical effect.
