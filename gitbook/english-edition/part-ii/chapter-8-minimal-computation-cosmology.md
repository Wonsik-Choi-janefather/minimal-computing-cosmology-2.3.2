# Chapter 8 Minimal Computation Cosmology

The first principle of Minimal Computation Cosmology is that reality has no reason to classically determine and store every unobserved detail. What is needed is not a state containing nothing, but a consistent rule connecting possible states to actual records. The target of minimization therefore includes not only the computational method but the size of source data, the order in which those data are read, temporal evolution, and the conditions for realizing reality.

The second principle is that minimizing computation does not simply mean minimizing the number of operations. It is structural minimization that jointly reduces the independent degrees of freedom to be represented, the information to be transported, the constraints to be maintained, the scope of recomputation after a modification, and the costs of initial loading and selective output.

C\_min = min\[L\_source + C\_read + C\_evolution + C\_render + C\_record] (2.3)

The third principle is locality. If a small change at one point required rewriting the entire description of the universe from the beginning, it would be difficult to obtain stable physical laws. Reality updates through local interactions and limited transport, while relations between distant regions must be connected under common rules.

The fourth principle is reuse. If mass, charge, spin, and position were produced in four unrelated factories and attached separately to a particle, coupling rules would proliferate explosively. Explanatory cost decreases when multiple observables appear jointly from one common state and common transport structure.

_ΔR(x) → ΔR(N\_local(x))_ (2.4)

From this perspective, laws are not the completed script of every event but compressed rules constraining realizable states. Initial conditions, boundary conditions, the vacuum, and coupling constants specify one instance of a universe. Entering observed values is legitimate, but inputs, structural derivations, and new predictions must be clearly distinguished.

## A Universe Read Once

The physical laws and constants in LAW are not commands fetched anew from outside at every moment. LAW determines the state space, symmetries, couplings, boundaries, evolution rules, and prime-port generation rules and constructs the dormant state; the primordial SOURCE impulse η then begins one history under that law. Thereafter, temporal evolution is computed autonomously from fixed LAW and the present state.

LAW ─defines→ (H\_WRRA, ρ\_dorm), SOURCE(η) ─opens→ ρ(0⁺), ρ(t+Δt)=T\_LAW,Δt\[ρ(t)]

Here, “once” does not literally mean that a computer outside the universe opens a file. It is a structural expression: laws and constants require no new external input at every time step and remain continuously effective within initialized dynamics. Only in this way does the universe become a self-contained execution system independent of external calls.

Minimal Computation Cosmology therefore treats the entire process of defining LAW, constructing the dormant state, opening the SOURCE, executing, outputting, and recording. It enters the description length L\_LAW of LAW, the length L\_SOURCE of the SOURCE boundary input, activation order C\_read, autonomous evolution C\_evolution, selective output C\_render, and recording C\_record as separate entries in the ledger.

## Information Is Provided in Six Ways

Asking for a physical quantity does not mean that its answer must always be stored numerically in SOURCE. The owner of an answer can be LAW, the dormant state, SOURCE, evolution, calibration, observation, or an open event. The WRRA information ledger distinguishes INPUT, DERIVED, CALIBRATED, OBSERVED, OPEN EVENT, and OPEN so that a value contained in LAW or fitted to observation is not mistaken for a prediction by SOURCE.

I\_LAW = {laws, constants, symmetries, couplings, boundaries}, I\_SOURCE = {η}, I\_derived(t) = E\_LAW^t\[I\_SOURCE, ρ\_dorm]

For example, the speed of light and Planck's constant are INPUTs in the LAW HEADER, not SOURCE. The present matter distribution and age of the universe are DERIVED from the initial state and dynamics. At the present stage, the electron mass and some coupling constants are CALIBRATED to observations. The location at which a particular electron is detected is an OPEN EVENT recorded when it occurs. The current SOURCE candidate is η; its physical magnitude and mapping to observables remain OPEN.

Crucially, an information grade is not a permanent identity. A mass value that is CALIBRATED today may later become DERIVED if a deeper generative rule is discovered that does not reuse the same observation. Scientific progress lies not in adding more values as inputs, but in independently deriving information that had remained input.

## How This Differs from Simulation Cosmology and the Standard Model

The three perspectives do not compete on the same layer. Simulation cosmology is not one unified standard theory but a family of philosophical and computational hypotheses holding that the universe could be executed on an external computer. The Standard Model is a validated quantum field theory that precisely describes particles and the strong, weak, and electromagnetic interactions. Minimal Computation Cosmology is a research program asking what information structure, execution order, and selective-output conditions allow an instance of a universe—including those laws and constants—to constitute reality.

In this comparison, Minimal Computation Cosmology does not claim to discard or replace the Standard Model. On the contrary, a successful renderer for our universe must reproduce the symmetries, particle content, and precision observations already explained by the Standard Model. The difference lies not in moving Standard-Model parameters into different symbols, but in extending the account into one computational ledger covering the size and ownership of those inputs, their read order, execution, and observational records.

“Minimal computation” is the name of a research program, not a completed theorem. What is counted as cost, how it is defined in quantum field theory and gravity, and how it relates to entropy, action, and circuit complexity remain open. This book does not hide that incompleteness as it proceeds to the next structure.

In this execution system, LAW owns the physical laws, constants, state space, dependency order, boundaries, SOURCE-to-state interface, and event instruments. The dormant state is constructed from LAW's invariance and constraint prescription. SOURCE is the boundary input η acting on this prepared structure; after booting, the Actual evolves autonomously according to LAW. Classical reality is selectively constituted when stable, shareable physical records form.

Universe = BOOT\[law, constants, schema] + EVOLVE\[Actual] + RENDER\[record query] + APPEND\[event]
