# Chapter 10 The Final Position of the Dimensional Filter

When many directions are possible, how do observable directions arise? The dimensional filter was the first structural answer to this question. It does not create dimensions from nothing; it is an operation that passes, among candidate relations in the source, the subset that can be transported consistently and recorded locally.

When filter F acts on a source state, K\_DF = F†F can be read as the effective kernel of the passing sector, and L\_DF = I − K\_DF as its nonpassing complement. This notation does not mean that a particular microscopic theory has already been completed; it is a ledger clarifying ownership of the visible and invisible sectors. 〔See “Kernel” in Appendix D〕

$$
K_DF = F†F, L_DF = I − K_DF
$$

_Equation (2.6)_

In the dimensional-filter study, numerical experiments supplied a three-dimensional structure and then reconstructed it stably through heat traces and spectral indicators. This is meaningful as a regression test of the algorithm, but it is not a prediction of three dimensions without input. Recovering an input structure and explaining why nature selected it are different claims.

Because of this distinction, a uniqueness proof is not an indispensable goal of the research. Our universe may be one among multiple realizable universes. The task of the dimensional filter is not to declare that only 3+1 dimensions are possible, but to make it computable which effective dimension appears stably under a given source and boundary condition.

$$
d_eff(E) = −2 · d log Tr(e^(−tK_DF)) / d log t
$$

_Equation (2.7)_

Dimension is now not an empty background box but the number of independent directions along which relations persist. This view no longer treats dimension as a prior stage separate from particles or forces; it handles dimension together with the structures of transport and observation.

The filter alone was not sufficient. Even after specifying the passing subspace, it did not determine how states move over time on that subspace. A Hamiltonian bridge was required to cross from a static selection operator into dynamical physics.

## From the Dimensional Filter to WRRA

The dimensional filter did not remain a separate theory competing with present-day WRRA. Dimension selection and representation conversion, the responsibilities of the early filter, were preserved as LAW modules inside WRRA. Added above them were LAW's dependency order, common transport, joint assignment of properties, autonomous evolution, selective realization, stable-record rules, and a separate SOURCE η interface.

Dimensional Filter ⊂ WRRA

The early one-dimensional point is likewise not fixed as a point in already visible physical space. It is the minimal address of LAW's relation structure. The dimensional filter inside WRRA transforms allowed relations among these addresses into visible three-dimensional geometry and phenotype directions, while the remaining WRRA modules execute the assignment of mass, charge, spin, and position to the same causal structure.
