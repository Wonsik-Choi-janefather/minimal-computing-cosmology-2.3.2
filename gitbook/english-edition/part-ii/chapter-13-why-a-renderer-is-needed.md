An actual detector, however, does not observe these four properties separately and then arbitrarily attach them to one particle.

A physical object is one event pattern to which mass, charge, spin, and position are jointly assigned. The object's identity must therefore be not the sum of several property lists but a joint phenotype maintained together under the same local observable algebra and the same translation action.

$$
Phenotype = Joint(m, q, s, x │ A_local, T_a)

_Equation (2.17)_

This is why a renderer is required between LAW and SOURCE, on one side, and observed reality on the other. A renderer is not an ornament that decorates possibility as an image. Here, phenotype is not simply a borrowed biological term. It means the result in which internal sources and relations appear as an actually observable physical object under a particular environment and boundary condition. Even with the same general renderer, different instance inputs and vacua can produce different phenotype universes.

WRRA's basic flow can therefore be summarized as LAW + SOURCE(η) → RENDERER → PHENOTYPE. RELATION and BOUNDARY provide the renderer's operating grammar and recording interface.
