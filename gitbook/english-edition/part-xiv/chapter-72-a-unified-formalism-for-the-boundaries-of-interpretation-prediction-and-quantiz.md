## 72.1 The Mathematical Boundary between Interpretation and Prediction

Let currently observed information be ℱ\_t^obs = σ(X\_t,R\_t,E\_t,Records\_t). If the conditional law of target Y concentrates at the single value G(ℱ\_t^obs) determined by present information, the task is exact interpretation. Long computation time or the absence of a known algorithm does not mean that information is open.

P(Y∈A|ℱₜobs)=1A(G(ℱₜobs)) a.s. ⇔ P(Y∈·|ℱₜobs)=δG(ℱₜobs) a.s.

Prediction begins when the conditional law is nondegenerate. For discrete output one can use H(Y|ℱ\_t^obs) > 0; for continuous output, instead of coordinate-dependent differential entropy one can use the diameter D\_t(ε) > 0 of a preregistered confidence set. In actual measurement, effective macroscopic definiteness is assessed by whether optimal conditional risk R\*(ℱ\_t) lies below the allowed error. This is not a declaration of fundamental determinism for the universe as a whole.

## 72.3 Correcting the Intuition of “From Two Dimensions to Three”

The original intuition “interpretation through the second order, prediction from the third” is incorrect if read as a theorem about polynomial degree. General cubic and quartic equations also have solutions by radicals, while lower-order dynamics can remain open because of external inputs. The Abel–Ruffini theorem says that general equations of degree five or higher cannot be solved by radicals alone, not that roots do not exist or cannot be computed.

The intuition survives in closure of relations. If Y = G(X,R) closes from present object X and realized residue R alone, the task is interpretation. If the next state requires an environmental or event term J absent from present information, so that X\_next = F(X,R,J) with J ∉ ℱ\_t^obs, a single future remains open. Here, the “third term” is not a number of variables or equation degree, but the role of context not yet supplied.

Rationality versus irrationality is likewise not the boundary. 1/3 has an infinite repeating decimal yet is finitely specified by two integers; √2, π, and e have infinite nonrepeating expansions yet can be calculated to any desired precision from finite rules. The key criteria are a finite generation rule, computational complexity, numerical conditioning, sensitivity to initial state, and closure of present information.

## 72.4 Four Problem Domains and Their Justified Outputs

In Domain I, finite-information reconstruction, the answer is fixed within finite input. Integer factorization and reading base sequences may be difficult, but they are not prediction of future events; their outputs are exact values and verification certificates. Domain II, infinite-structure closure, must compress a defined infinite static structure—such as the zeta function and Riemann Hypothesis—into a finite proof. Verification to a finite height is not a global proof.

In Domain III, micro–macro amplification, laws and ideal initial conditions may be closed while small observational errors grow through Lyapunov amplification. The justified outputs for the three-body problem are a finite-time trajectory and a predictability horizon. Here “micro” first means a difference in classical state below observational resolution, not necessarily a quantum scale.

In Domain IV, information-opening transitions, the next environment or event is absent from present information or not yet realized. Cell fate, tropical-cyclone tracks, and economic change include this domain to differing degrees. The justified output is not one number but a conditional distribution and scenarios whose calibration, sharpness, and tail risk have been validated. Actual problems can occupy two or more domains.

## 72.5 Quantization Thresholds and Accumulation of Binary Records

A small difference does not always separate zero from one. Under threshold map b\_θ(x) = 1{x ≥ θ}, perturbation δ changes the record only when x and x+δ fall on opposite sides of θ. The probability mass concentrated in the threshold corridor must be measured first.

bθ(x+δ)≠bθ(x) ⇔ (x−θ)(x+δ−θ)<0

P\[bθ(X+δ)≠bθ(X)]≤P(|X−θ|≤ε)=2fX(θ)ε+o(ε)

Across multiple steps, Jacobian products amplify differences as δX\_n ≈ J\_{n−1}⋯J₀δX₀ + Σ\_jJ\_{n−1}⋯J\_{j+1}η\_j, while repeated exposure to thresholds accumulates opportunities for branching. A union bound is only an upper bound, not proof that branching probability must approach one. Correlations, saturation, and nonlinear feedback must be modeled separately.

## 72.6 From Quantum Events to Phenotypic Records

Unitary density-operator evolution is deterministic, while recorded outcomes follow the Born distribution. A quantum instrument separates the average channel from the event conditioned on outcome y. Decoherence through environmental entanglement suppresses local interference in a pointer basis, but does not by itself derive one ontologically actual outcome.

Dtr(ρ₀,ρ₁)=½||ρ₀−ρ₁||₁, Perr,opt=(1−Dtr)/2

q\_Φ,loc is the first channel-specific minimum event equivalence class satisfying D\_tr ≥ 1−2ε and τ\_rec ≥ τ\*. q\_Φ,obj additionally requires environmental redundancy R\_δ. It is neither a particle, ℏ, nor a universal minimum energy; when resources are multidimensional or nonmonotonic, it must be defined as a Pareto minimum under a preregistered ordering.

The macro–micro boundary is therefore not determined by one size. A sufficiently isolated macroscopic degree of freedom can retain interference, while one microscopic event can immediately become a macroscopic record under strong amplification. In this book, macroscopic definiteness means effective historical fixation of a record with distinguishability, persistence, and environmental redundancy.

## 72.7 Reading Mathematics, Life, Meteorology, and Economics by One Criterion

Factorization requires factors and a multiplication certificate; the Riemann Hypothesis, a global theorem, proof, or counterexample; the three-body problem, finite-time paths and horizons; meteorology and economics, ensembles and conditional scenarios; and DNA and cells, separation of present functional interpretation from future phenotype distributions. The question and information set—not the field—determine the output.

The Riemann Hypothesis is not future prediction but closure of an infinite structure. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22346428 makes this distinction stricter. A record that no counterexample was found up to a finite height, in a finite sample, through finite jets and finite precision, is analysis of that scope; it does not certify absence from an unopened infinite tail. If decay of the far-distance Gram remainder in the earlier Wonsik–Jeongin filter holds under rigorous assumptions, it can be a partial result closing the far-tail budget. A finite-to-mesoscopic corridor in which diagonal growth uniformly cancels nearby-scale negative off-diagonal terms, however, requires a separate global theorem. This does not mean absolute unprovability of RH or independence from ZFC.

The preceding audits in biology, neuroscience, meteorology, and economics reached the same conclusion. Already formed sequences, cell states, connectomes, present analysis fields, and economic indicators can be interpreted; when new environments, interactions, policies, and collective decisions enter the future, a single-value contract becomes inappropriate. New observations and interventions convert some uncertainty into present information, so the boundary is not a fixed size line but moves with information and experiment.

## 72.8 A WRRA Application Procedure That Preserves Failure

The application sequence is: ① fix whether the objective is interpreting present structure or predicting the next state; ② create a time-and-provenance ledger for X, R, E, J, and Records; ③ test information ownership using state-only, equal-length raw history, time-shift, shuffle, and nonlinear baselines; ④ classify the four closure domains; and ⑤ preregister the output contract and stopping condition among value, proof, trajectory, and distribution.

If a residue effect disappears after controls for raw history and a strong nonlinear current state, ownership moves to the environment. If conditional width remains stably above zero even under the hypothesis that present information closes the next state, point prediction stops and a distribution is output. If an argument about an infinite structure depends only on finite-range calculations or assumes a cancellation equivalent to its conclusion, no global proof is claimed.

A WRRA-specific physical effect requires first freezing quantum instruments, open-system dynamics, detector and thermal noise, standard non-Markovian memory, and state uncertainty. It is insufficient for two preparations with different histories merely to look identical at one present instant; Δ\_history ≠ 0 must remain reproducible even after multitime predictive equivalence at the process-tensor level.

## 72.9 A Task-Relative Boundary Classifier

Declare task T = (I\_t,Q,t\_Q,ε,B,L) and form the compatible-state set Ω(I\_t) = {ω : ω is compatible with the declared records, constraints, and errors}. Target diameter D\_Q(I\_t) = diam{Q(ω) : ω ∈ Ω(I\_t)} and dimensionless diameter d\_Q = D\_Q/s\_Q measure whether present information effectively fixes Q within allowed error. Ω is an epistemic compatible set within a selected model family, not an ontological catalog of possible physical universes.

Reconstructibility alone is insufficient. The classifier jointly examines whether R\*(Q|I\_t) = inf\_aE\[L(a,Q)|I\_t] lies below the task-specific criterion; whether C\_Q(ε) fits within budget B; how small information perturbations move the target; the threshold-crossing probability and Lyapunov or Jacobian amplification; and whether conditional uncertainty remains in an unrecorded Q.

Five contracts are selected from boundary vector B\_Q(I\_t) = (d\_Q,R\*,C\_Q,A\_Q,H\_Q,q\_Φ). Ⅰ Interpretation outputs a stably reconstructed value and uncertainty; Ⅱ Computation, an algorithm, complexity, and approximation for a fixed answer; Ⅲ Prediction, a distribution, interval, or ensemble; Ⅳ Proof Closure, a finite proof, counterexample, or conditional theorem; and Ⅴ Open Problem, a declaration of nonclosure and requirements for additional information. Classification is not forced unless target, information, error, budget, and loss have been declared.

## 72.10 Record Quality and Reclassification before and after an Event

Record quality is q\_Φ = w\_DD\_rec+w\_PP\_rec+w\_RR\_red, with weights summing to one. D\_rec represents distinguishability of alternative outcomes, P\_rec persistence for the required time, and R\_red redundancy across independent channels or environmental fragments. q\_Φ and threshold q\* are not universal constants or particle sizes, but task-relative scales calibrated for a field and apparatus before results are seen.

Before a record forms, the justified description of an event may be a nondegenerate conditional distribution. After amplification and threshold crossing make an outcome distinguishable, persistent, and redundant, the task of reading that record becomes interpretation. This framework does not claim that decoherence automatically proves a single-outcome ontology, nor does it combine quantum probability, classical chaos, observational noise, and model error into one cause.

## 72.11 Preregistration, Validation, and Prevention of Misclassification

Interpretation validates reconstruction error and record stability across observers, devices, and repeated measurements. Prediction compares calibration, coverage, sharpness, and strictly proper scores with baselines on unused future data, new regions, and new cohorts. A nominal 90% prediction interval must show approximately 90% coverage in the declared validation environment; a post hoc widened interval does not count as prospective prediction.

Shortcuts such as “quantum always means prediction,” “determinism always means interpretation,” “complexity means prediction,” and “infinity means prediction” are discarded. A recorded quantum result can be interpreted; deterministic chaos requires distributional prediction beyond a finite-precision horizon; a fixed difficult answer is a computation problem; and an infinite static proposition such as the Riemann Hypothesis is a proof-closure problem. The unit of classification is the information–target contract, not the object's name.
