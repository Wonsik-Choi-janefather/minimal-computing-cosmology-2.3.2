# Chapter 14 The Multiple Layers of Quantum Realization

There is more than one route to a quantum realization of the Bessel common carrier. The research lineage distinguishes three levels: a finite Jacobi runtime, a single generalized-field spectral runtime, and a warped defect-holographic parent. 〔See “Jacobi Matrix” in Appendix D〕

_Jacobi runtime → spectral field → warped defect parent_ (3.7)

The finite Jacobi runtime approximates a continuous spectrum with a finite matrix and discrete states. It is the most conservative, computable, and local quantum runtime. It is well suited to numerical matching of poles, residues, and responses in a restricted energy window, but does not preserve an exact infinite continuum as such.

A single generalized field preserves a continuum compressed into one nonlocal or pseudodifferential spectral symbol. It can represent the target two-point function without introducing infinitely many independent charged fields, but closure of interacting subgraphs, gauge completion, and renormalization become separate thresholds.

The warped defect parent is one step more geometric. A local field in higher dimensions produces a Bessel-type spectrum at a boundary or defect. It can provide ownership of bulk causality and positive spectral compression, but this does not mean that the higher dimension is a fundamental dimension of nature.

Even if the three runtimes reproduce the same positive two-point spectral target within a restricted matching window, they are not physically identical theories. They differ in number of fields, locality, gauge completion, ultraviolet cost, and the Hilbert space in which unitarity is defined.

$$
G₁(E) ≈ G₂(E) on E∈W ⇏ Theory₁ = Theory₂
$$

_Equation (3.8)_

The statement that “quantum realization is complete” must therefore also be made layer by layer. Two-point reproduction, interaction consistency, relative closure at one loop, all-loop completion, and Standard-Model embedding are distinct thresholds. The present research closes some layers but does not complete quantum gravity as a whole.
