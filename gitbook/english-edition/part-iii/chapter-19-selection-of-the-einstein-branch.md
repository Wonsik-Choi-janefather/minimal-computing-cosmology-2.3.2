# Chapter 19 Selection of the Einstein Branch

Even if renderer path independence demands consistency of hypersurface deformation, the possible gravitational dynamics are not fixed uniquely without additional assumptions. Hojman–Kuchař–Teitelboim-type conditions must be stated to obtain the Einstein branch.

The central assumptions are local metric canonical variables, the standard spatial-diffeomorphism action, reversibility, the absence of additional gravitational canonical fields, dependence at most quadratic in momenta, and no more than two spatial derivatives.

Under these conditions, if the Hamiltonian and momentum constraints realize the hypersurface-deformation algebra as first-class, the Einstein dynamics of general relativity is selected as a conditional low-energy branch. A cosmological-constant term may be allowed; its concrete value is a separate vacuum and observational question.

$$
Recovered path equivalence + HKT assumptions ⇒ Einstein branch
$$

_Equation (3.21)_

The correct logical direction is therefore not “path independence ⇒ Einstein equations.” It is “path equivalence ⇒ a consistent deformation and gauge structure,” followed by “that structure + HKT-type assumptions ⇒ the Einstein branch.”

Other branches become possible if additional scalars, higher derivatives, nonlocal variables, or different canonical content are allowed. This does not mean WRRA denies Einstein gravity; it clarifies the domain of applicability and the selection assumptions of Einstein gravity.

The position of the five-dimensional parent is organized in the same way. It is a candidate chiral and spectral dilation of the matter renderer, not the ultimate LAW or renderer. The low-energy gravity of our universe may follow the Einstein branch even while the renderer's fundamental structure is broader.
