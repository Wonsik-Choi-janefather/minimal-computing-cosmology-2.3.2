# Chapter 66 WRRA Life Model 0: The First Living System

Conclusion of this chapter: The first life is not identical to a modern autonomous cell or LUCA. WRRA Life Model 0 operationally defines the first life as “the state at which a bounded chemical system first maintained heritable functional closure and achieved an expected number of functional daughter lineages R\_life > 1.” This definition and mathematical threshold are executable, but the actual first sequence, location, membrane, and historical path remain OPEN.

## 66.1 Separating the First Life from LUCA

DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22291862 distinguishes chemical replicators, protocells, the first living lineage, autonomous cells, and LUCA. A chemical structure that grows and divides may lack heredity, while a bounded protocell may fail to preserve function across generations. Conversely, the first life may have produced a selectable lineage despite heavy dependence on external geochemical cycles. LUCA is the last common ancestral population of extant cellular life, not the first transition to life itself.

A 2024 phylogenomic study inferred LUCA to have been an already complex, prokaryote-grade anaerobic acetogen approximately 4.2 billion years ago. This result supports the logical distinction between first life and LUCA, although every inferred LUCA trait and date depends on phylogenetic models and fossil and isotope calibrations.

## 66.2 Minimal Functional Closure of Five Owners

A minimal candidate must jointly possess: ① information preserving a function-related sequence or composition; ② a catalyst accelerating closed reactions; ③ a boundary concentrating components while passing supplies; ④ energy coupling maintaining reactions away from equilibrium; and ⑤ allocation transmitting sufficient function to daughter lineages. If any one repeatedly fails, a one-time chemical event cannot become a living lineage.

FIRST LIVING LINEAGE ⇔ bounded system ∧ heritable functional variation ∧ Rlife>1

This equation is not proof of a universal definition of life, but the assessment rule adopted by this research. To avoid excluding viruses, symbionts, nonsequence inheritance, or extraterrestrial chemistry, it measures boundary, functional inheritance, and lineage persistence rather than “autonomy.”

## 66.3 The Exact Threshold of Copying Error and Non-Copying Closure

Let L be the number of independently copied function-related sites, f the mean probability that one site is copied without a function-destroying error, and K the non-copying probability that boundary, catalyst, energy, and allocation jointly succeed in one daughter lineage. In the declared baseline with independent factors and two daughters, the probability q that one daughter maintains functional closure and the expected number of functional daughters are as follows.

q=Kfᴸ, Rlife=2q=2Kfᴸ

The critical copying fidelity at R\_life = 1 is f\_c = (2K)⁻¹ᐟᴸ, and a physically possible solution f ≤ 1 exists only when K > 1/2. If K ≤ 1/2, even perfect copying f = 1 fails to exceed one lineage on average; improving heredity accuracy alone cannot compensate for a frequently failing renderer.

This result is EXACT within the declared binary-fission, mean-f, independent-factorization model. If real errors have position-specific effects, membrane and energy failures are correlated, daughters are asymmetric, or environments vary between generations, q = Kfᴸ cannot be applied directly and the offspring kernel must be remeasured.

## 66.4 Conditional Numerical Scenarios and the Information Budget

K = 0.61965, the product of boundary 0.90, catalyst 0.85, energy 0.90, and allocation 0.90, is a sensitivity input rather than a measurement of the early Earth. At L = 100, f\_c = 0.997857, and at f = 0.999, R\_life = 1.121. For the same K, critical lengths at f = 0.98, 0.99, 0.995, and 0.999 are approximately 10.6, 21.3, 42.8, and 214.4 sites, respectively.

Lmax=ln\[1/(2K)]/ln f (0\<f<1, K>1/2)

This calculation shows that inherited information must be short at low copying fidelity and that the environment can supply many functions. Because the numbers change with site independence and the definition of a “function-destroying error,” however, it is a MODEL-CONDITIONAL BUDGET, not a universal limit on biological information.

## 66.5 The Environmental Renderer and Evidence-Compatible Candidates

The first lineage need not internally produce every monomer, gradient, and division condition. Wet–dry cycles, temperature fluctuations, photochemistry, mineral surfaces, and redox gradients may have supplied concentration, activation, and mechanical division, while evolution gradually internalized those functions as catalysts, membrane machinery, and metabolism. The selected unit in this case is not the environment itself but a bounded lineage whose internal state changes descendant production.

L₀=(RNA-like information)+(short catalytic peptides)+(permeable fatty-acid boundary)+(repeatable energy cycle)

An RNA-like informational polymer, short catalytic peptides, permeable fatty-acid vesicles, and repeatable energy cycles form one candidate combination compatible with present evidence. Common precursor chemistry, vesicle capture, growth, and division, nonenzymatic RNA synthesis in model protocells, peptide–vesicle coupling, and adaptive evolution of synthetic protocells support the respective modules. Seed-free, self-sustained abiogenesis combining all of them, or the historical first lineage, has not been experimentally reconstructed.

## 66.6 Populations over Individuals, Lineages over One-Time Events

Because early compartments may have frequently leaked, fused, and exchanged material horizontally, a population with state distribution π\_t(x) and offspring kernel B(y|x) is more appropriate than a “single first individual.” Chemical reproduction itself creates the next-generation distribution.

πt₊₁(y) ∝ ∫B(y|x)πt(x)dx

Selection does not introduce an external selector; it summarizes differences in offspring distributions produced by different states. Lineages with spectral radius greater than one remain relatively represented, but the kernel of the actual early environment has not yet been measured.

## 66.7 Assessment and Frozen Experiment

The experimental unit must link one mother compartment to two daughters and track both daughters through their next reproduction. The mother sequence and copy number, catalyst and peptide composition, membrane composition and geometry, energy and supply history, both daughters' compositions, and the next division of each daughter are recorded together. L, the functional-error definition of f, components of K, observation window, and criteria for P₀/P₁/P₂ are frozen before results are inspected.

Chapter conclusion WRRA Life Model 0 does not assert “which molecule came first,” but supplies a minimum measurement boundary for the transition from chemistry to a living lineage. The core is the joint intergenerational closure of five functions, not information alone or a membrane alone. This biological application makes the universal-execution-grammar hypothesis of minimal computation concrete, but does not count as physical validation of WRRA cosmology.
