# E-1 SOURCE and Dormant State

## 1. Canonical relation of the opening degree of freedom · Reprint of the canonical relation in main-text Eq. (3.1)

$$\left\lbrack \Omega,P_{\Omega} \right\rbrack = i\hslash$$

**Meaning** Declares that opening coordinate Ω and its conjugate momentum P\_Ω form a quantum canonical pair.\
**Scope** An effective theory that takes Ω and P\_Ω as fundamental WRRA degrees of freedom.\
**Grade** STRUCTURAL\
**Basis** 10.5281/zenodo.22182131

## 2. Parameter-free dormant-state prescription · Reprint of main-text Eq. (3.2)

$$\rho_{dorm} = F\left( H_{potential},G,\mathcal{A},\mathcal{C} \right),\quad\text{free coefficients} = 0$$

**Meaning** The pre-impulse state must be fixed by the potential, symmetry, observable algebra, and constraints; arbitrary initial values must not be hidden within it.\
**Scope** When trace normalization of the invariant positive trace-class cone forms a singleton.\
**Grade** CONDITIONAL; the minimal-SOURCE claim fails if uniqueness fails\
**Basis** 10.5281/zenodo.22182219

## 3. SOURCE impulse operator · Reprint of the SOURCE operator in main-text Eq. (2.1)

$$U_{S} = \exp\left( \frac{i\eta\Omega}{\hslash} \right)$$

**Meaning** One finite boundary action acts on the opening coordinate. η is not a particle-by-particle list but a boundary datum that begins one history.\
**Scope** Ω is self-adjoint and η is a finite boundary map.\
**Grade** CANONICAL IDENTITY\
**Basis** 10.5281/zenodo.22182131

## 4. Translation of opening momentum · Reprint of the momentum translation in main-text Eq. (2.1)

$$U_{S}^{\dagger}P_{\Omega}U_{S} = P_{\Omega} + \eta,\quad\Delta P_{\Omega} = \eta$$

**Meaning** By the Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff identity, the SOURCE action translates P\_Ω by exactly η.\
**Scope** When \[Ω,P\_Ω]=iħ and η is a c-number.\
**Grade** EXACT\
**Basis** 10.5281/zenodo.22182131
