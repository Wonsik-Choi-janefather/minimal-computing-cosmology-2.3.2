# E-2 Autonomous Evolution and Visible Reality

## 5. Autonomous evolution of the full WRRA state · Reprint of the Actual-evolution part of main-text Eq. (2.2)

$$\rho_{actual}(\tau) = e^{- iH_{WRRA}\tau/\hslash}U_{S}\rho_{dorm}U_{S}^{\dagger}e^{+ iH_{WRRA}\tau/\hslash}$$

**Meaning** After the one-time SOURCE, the fixed WRRA Hamiltonian evolves the entire Actual through time.\
**Scope** When H\_WRRA is self-adjoint and a closed-system unitary description is used.\
**Grade** FORMAL EXACT; the completed H\_WRRA is OPEN\
**Basis** 10.5281/zenodo.22182131

## 6. Restriction to visible reality · Reprint of the visible-restriction part of main-text Eq. (2.2)

$$\rho_{reality}(\tau) = \mathcal{P}_{vis}\left\lbrack \rho_{actual}(\tau) \right\rbrack$$

**Meaning Of the entire Actual, only the portion exposed to the observable algebra physically accessible to an observer becomes visible reality.**\
**Scope In a tensor-factor Schrödinger picture, when P\_vis is a CPTP map such as a partial trace. For a general observable algebra, when it is defined as the dual map of a unital CP map in the Heisenberg picture or of a conditional expectation whose existence has been proved.**\
**Grade STRUCTURAL / CONDITIONAL**\
**Basis 10.5281/zenodo.22182131**

## 7. Born probability of an observed event · Reprint of main-text Eq. (2.3)

$$p(o,\tau) = Tr\left\lbrack E_{o}\rho_{reality}(\tau) \right\rbrack$$

**Meaning** Calculates the probability of outcome o corresponding to POVM element E\_o in visible reality.\
**Scope** A quantum measurement with E\_o ≥ 0 and Σ\_o E\_o = I.\
**Grade** STANDARD QUANTUM IDENTITY\
**Basis** 10.5281/zenodo.22182131
