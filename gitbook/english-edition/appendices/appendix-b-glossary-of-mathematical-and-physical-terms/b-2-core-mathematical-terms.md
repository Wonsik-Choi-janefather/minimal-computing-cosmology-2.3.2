# B-2 Core Mathematical Terms

## Function

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** A mapping that produces an output from an input according to a fixed rule. One may think of a vending-machine button and its drink, although the input and output may themselves be numbers, vectors, or functions.

**In this book** The most basic language for writing how input information about the universe changes into an output state.

**Caution** A function does not necessarily mean a physical device operating through time.

## Bessel function

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** A special function that arises naturally when calculating waves spreading from a center, as in a circle or cylinder. If the sine wave of a guitar string is the language of linear vibration, the Bessel function is closer to the language of circular membranes, cylinders, and radial waves.

**In this book** It is used in WRRA’s common-carrier and 15-channel models to express time- and distance-dependent responses and to calculate the conditions under which rank opens or a spectrum settles.

**Caution** The appearance of a Bessel function in an equation does not by itself prove that the universe is cylindrical or that the proposed carrier physically exists.

## Riemann zeta function

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** Initially defined as the sum of terms of the form 1/nˢ and extended analytically over a wider region of the complex plane. Because it is also connected to a product over primes, it compactly contains the multiplicative structure of the natural numbers.

**In this book** In this book it is used as a reproducible mathematical seed for constructing a finite mode window and SOURCE candidates.

**Caution** It has not been proved that the zeta function is the universe’s unique initial state or that nature must read the zeta function.

## Zero

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** An input that makes a function’s value zero—analogous to locating where a mountain’s elevation meets sea level.

**In this book** A zero of the zeta function or of a determinant marks a candidate point at which a particular mode disappears or a new condition becomes satisfied.

**Caution** A mathematical zero and the disappearance of a physical particle are not the same statement. A separate correspondence rule is needed to connect them.

## Complex number and phase

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** A complex number expresses not only magnitude but also phase, its angle of rotation. Waves of equal magnitude can reinforce or cancel one another when their phases differ.

**In this book** Used to describe interference and mixing among channels, lossless angles, and the arrangement of zeta modes.

**Caution** Here phase is not the everyday word for a vague mood or stage; it is mathematically defined angular information.

## Operator

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** A rule that transforms an entire state or function into another state or function, rather than acting on a single number. It is analogous to a translation rule acting on an entire sentence.

**In this book, it is the basic mathematical unit for expressing how LAW and WRRA update relations and carry them across boundaries after SOURCE η.**

**Caution** Writing down an operator does not mean that a physical operating mechanism in nature has been discovered.

## Eigenvalue and eigenvector

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** An eigenvector is a special state whose direction is preserved under a transformation while only its magnitude changes by a fixed ratio; that ratio is the eigenvalue.

**In this book** Used to locate stable modes, allowed energies, and spectral positions.

**Caution** For an eigenvalue to equal an observed particle mass or energy, its units and correspondence formula must be validated.

## Spectrum

**Status Established mathematical and physical concept**

**Plain meaning** The collection of eigenvalues allowed by a system. Just as a prism spreads light into colors, a spectrum unfolds a complex system into its possible vibration or energy components.

**In this book** A central output for comparing which modes and particle-like representations WRRA permits.

**Caution** Obtaining a similarly shaped spectrum is different from independently predicting the particle values of the Standard Model.

## Mode

**Status Established mathematical and physical concept**

**Plain meaning** One pattern in which a system can vibrate or change independently, analogous to different notes of a musical instrument.

**In this book, it is a computational unit used to construct dormant-state seeds and LAW transmission structures within a finite computational window such as the 6,369 modes.**

**Caution** The number of modes is not automatically the number of particles or the total information content of the universe.

## Matrix

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** A tool that arranges multiple numbers in a rectangle so that relations and transformations can be calculated together.

**In this book** It enables channel coupling, transitions, mixing, rank, and eigenvalues to be handled by finite calculations.

## Jacobi matrix

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** Usually a tridiagonal matrix with nonzero entries only on the main diagonal and its two adjacent diagonals. It compactly represents a long chain connected through nearest neighbors.

**In this book** It transfers a continuous spectrum into a finite-chain model, enabling numerical calculation and error testing.

**Caution** Because a finite Jacobi matrix approximates an original infinite or continuous system, truncation-error and convergence tests are required.

## Determinant

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** A single number indicating how much a matrix expands or distorts spatial volume. If it is zero, some direction has been compressed so that information cannot be resolved independently.

**In this book** Zeros of the 15-channel Bessel determinant are used to locate candidate rank-opening points.

**Caution** A determinant zero alone does not establish matter generation; dynamics and an observational correspondence are also required.

## Rank and rank opening

**Status Established linear-algebra concept + interpretation in this book**

**Plain meaning** Rank counts how many mutually independent information directions a matrix contains. If rank increases from a lower value, degrees of freedom that were previously bound together can move independently.

**In this book** “Rank opening” denotes an event in which the channels of the common carrier resolve into independent degrees of freedom and the condition for matter generation opens.

**Caution** The computational fact of increasing rank must be distinguished from its physical interpretation as the actual Big Bang or particle generation.

## Kernel

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** Two meanings are common, depending on context: the set of states that an operator maps to zero, or a function describing how an input’s influence spreads to an output.

**In this book** This book mainly uses the second meaning—a rule linking input and output in interaction, transmission, and many-body calculations.

**Caution** The intended meaning must be checked sentence by sentence; it is different from the kernel of a computer operating system.

## Propagator

**Status Established quantum-field-theory concept**

**Plain meaning** A mathematical function describing how influence at one point or time is transmitted to another point or time.

**In this book** It is used to test whether the common carrier’s response and electron interactions continue consistently through time and space.

**Caution** A propagator is not itself the trajectory of a single particle flying along a classical path.

## Green’s function

**Status Established mathematical and physical concept**

**Plain meaning** A fundamental response function showing how a system responds to an impulse applied at one point. Responses to multiple impulses can be calculated by superposition.

**In this book** It is used to solve transmission problems under linear response and boundary conditions and is closely related to a propagator.

**Caution** Depending on context, Green’s functions and propagators may coincide or may be related concepts differing by constants or boundary treatment.

## Hilbert space

**Status Established mathematical concept**

**Plain meaning** A complete space in which vector lengths and angles can be defined and limits can be handled stably. It may be regarded as the stage on which quantum states reside.

**In this book** Used in attempts to treat filters, modes, and states within one rigorous vector space.

**Caution** An abstract state space is not identical to physical space.

## Hamiltonian

**Status Established physical concept**

**Plain meaning** An operator that contains a system’s energy structure and, in quantum mechanics, generates how its state changes through time.

**In this book** It becomes central when extending the dimension filter’s static selection rule into an executable model with time evolution.

**Caution** Calling a matrix a Hamiltonian requires checking conditions such as self-adjointness, units, stability, and a physical state space.
