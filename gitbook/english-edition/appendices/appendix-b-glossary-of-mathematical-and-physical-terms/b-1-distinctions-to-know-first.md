# B-1 Distinctions to Know First

**Established concept** A term already defined in standard mathematics or physics, such as Bessel function, zeta function, eigenvalue, or Hamiltonian. These concepts were not invented by this book.

**Model concept in this book** A term proposed in the author’s research lineage, such as WRRA, SOURCE–RELATION–BOUNDARY, or selective realization. It may prove useful, but it is not yet terminology accepted as a standard theory.

**Hypothetical connection** An interpretation that connects an established mathematical structure to the way the actual universe is generated and observed. Mathematical calculability alone does not prove physical reality.
