## B-8. Additional WRRA 2.0 Terms

prime SOURCE: an independent arithmetic marker irreducible to a synthetic product structure. It is not automatically identified with a physical particle or energy.

composite relation port: a potential relational address specified by the valuation incidence of a composite integer.

fixed-present ownership scaled simplex: a present-ownership state space satisfying q\_k ≥ 0, finite support, and 1ᵀq\_k=Q₀. Only p\_k=q\_k/Q₀ is a coordinate on a probability simplex when Q₀>0; q\_k is not a quantum density matrix.

WJNS arithmetic scale gate: an arithmetic selection device that connects the registry layer and execution layer using multiplicative scales and a completed reflection structure.

E\_lift / R\_phys: E\_lift lifts a classical registry/carrier state into the quantum Actual, while R\_phys is a physicalization map connecting the Actual to dimensionful fields, geometry, and an observable algebra. Both are currently OPEN.

WRRA-Cell residue h: a slow candidate state that retains effects of earlier stimuli in a finite-dimensional present. Before a measurable physical correspondence is identified, it is indistinguishable from a latent state or exponential smoother.

identifiability gate: a rule that closes claims not supported by the supplied data before calculation. Static Perturb-seq alone does not identify temporal residue, recovery, or rechallenge in the same cell.

partial non-confirmation: a state in which a preregistered prediction was unsupported in the testable portion, but the data lacked scope to judge the entire hypothesis. It is neither success nor comprehensive falsification.

economic residue: a slow candidate state through which effects of earlier shocks remain in present debt, credit supply, employment practices, investment delays, risk management, institutional trust, and related structures, influencing the next economic state. It is not automatically identical to a single consumer-sentiment index.

crisis-memory regime: an analysis interval combining an official recession with a predeclared subsequent observation window to test whether slow shock effects differ from tranquil periods. The window length cannot be promoted to a universal constant after inspecting the data.

temporal placebo: a control test that deliberately shifts a present-residue candidate into the past to determine whether present alignment is genuinely more explanatory. If an older proxy performs better, the temporal specificity of current residue is not identified.

real-time vintage: the value of an economic indicator actually available to a forecaster at the time, before later revisions. Using only the current final revision can introduce hindsight.

track-derived residue: a variable compressing the prior motion, acceleration, and intensity change of a tropical-cyclone center into finite present EWMA coordinates. It is not identical to the environmental field itself and requires evidence that it is an independent physical state variable.

environmental-field ownership: the hypothesis that the present environmental field—including steering flow, pressure field, wind shear, moisture, ocean heat, and land interaction—may be the true owner of dynamical information, rather than the trace remaining in the observed track.

forward validation: validation that trains on past data and performs model selection and final testing only in later periods. It blocks leakage of future information but does not eliminate regime change itself.

storm-cluster bootstrap: an uncertainty-estimation method that resamples whole observation intervals, rather than incorrectly counting multiple time points within one tropical cyclone as independent samples.

predictive non-uniqueness: the condition in which a unique future cannot be recovered from a restricted observed present. This alone does not prove ontological indeterminism.

information closure: the property that a declared set of present information determines the target as a single value. It is distinct from high computational cost.

conditional Dirac law: the condition in which, given present information, the target’s conditional distribution concentrates entirely at one value. This is the exact interpretation criterion of WRRA v2.1.

information-open transition: a transition whose conditional distribution is nondegenerate because an environmental, event, or boundary input required for the next state is absent from present information or has not yet been realized.

finite proof compression: a proof that closes a global proposition through finite invariants, symmetry, contradiction, or recursive structure instead of checking infinitely many cases one by one.

threshold corridor: a region in which the state lies close enough to a quantization threshold that a small perturbation can change a 0/1 or other discrete record.

effective macro-determinacy: a state in which conditional risk is below the tolerance under a preregistered resolution and loss function, and the record remains stable. It is not identical to fundamental determinism.

output contract: a rule that fixes in advance the legitimate output form—value, certificate, global proof, finite-time trajectory, ensemble, or conditional distribution—according to the problem’s closure type.
