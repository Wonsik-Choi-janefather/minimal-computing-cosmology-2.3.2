# B-3 Core Physics Terms

## Field

**Status Established physical concept**

**Plain meaning** A structure in which a physical quantity is assigned to every point in space and time. Just as a temperature field has a temperature at each location, a quantum field assigns states and operators to points.

**In this book** It provides the background for understanding a particle not merely as an independent little ball, but as a local excitation or phenotype of a field.

## Particle

**Status Established physical concept**

**Plain meaning** In modern physics, a particle may be represented, depending on context, as a local detection event, an excitation of a quantum field, or a state with particular mass, charge, and spin.

**In this book** A representative example of the observable phenotypes that WRRA seeks to produce.

**Caution** The word “particle” does not invariably mean a classical tiny bead.

## Quantum state and superposition

**Status Established quantum-mechanical concept**

**Plain meaning** A quantum state contains the possible measurement outcomes for a system and their probabilities. Superposition is not merely ignorance about several classical possibilities; it represents them together in a single state vector.

**In this book** This is the starting point of the selective-realization problem: how possible states appear as one actual record.

**Caution** Superposition need not immediately be interpreted as meaning that every different universe physically exists.

## Measurement and observation

**Status Established physical concept + interpretive problem**

**Plain meaning** Measurement is the process by which a system interacts with an apparatus and environment to create a readable record. It does not mean only the act of a conscious human looking.

**In this book** WRRA extends this into the hypothesis that a physical record query triggers a phenotypic output—that is, a renderer.

**Caution** Neither the claim that reality exists only when observed nor the claim that human consciousness creates the universe is a necessary conclusion of this book.

## Gauge symmetry

**Status Established physical concept**

**Plain meaning** The redundancy by which the same physical state can be represented in multiple mathematical notations, together with the principle that observables must remain unchanged under those representational changes.

**In this book** It tests whether charges and interactions are consistent and whether a choice of notation alters predictions.

**Caution** “Gauge” here differs from the everyday meaning of an instrument dial or simple measurement scale.

## Chirality

**Status Established particle-physics concept**

**Plain meaning** The property that two mirror-image structures, like left and right hands, cannot be superposed by rotation alone. The weak interaction treats left- and right-handed components differently.

**In this book** A central condition in asking how the 15-channel common carrier could permit a matter structure of one handedness.

**Caution** Finding a geometric asymmetry alone does not complete the Standard Model’s exact chiral interactions.

## Gauge anomaly

**Status Established quantum-field-theory concept**

**Plain meaning** A phenomenon in which a symmetry of the classical theory breaks during quantum calculation. If a gauge anomaly remains, probability conservation and consistency of the theory can fail.

**In this book** It checks whether charge combinations in the first-generation Standard Model inventory can cancel anomalies.

**Caution** Anomaly cancellation is necessary, but it is not sufficient to show that the particle content was uniquely derived from nature.

## Ward–Takahashi identity

**Status Established quantum-field-theory relation**

**Plain meaning** A relation requiring propagators and interaction vertices to match consistently because of gauge symmetry.

**In this book** It checks whether the transmission formula and vertex preserve the same symmetry in electron gauge closure.

**Caution** Even when the identity closes, the transverse structure and the value of the coupling constant do not automatically become unique.

## Standard Model of particle physics

**Status Established physical theory**

**Plain meaning** A quantum field theory that describes quarks, leptons, gauge bosons, the Higgs, and the electromagnetic, weak, and strong interactions with extraordinary accuracy. It does not include gravity.

**In this book** This book reads the Standard Model as the “phenotypic grammar” of observable particles and interactions, and asks whether WRRA can be a candidate generative structure beneath it.

**Caution** This does not mean that the Standard Model is merely a classification table with labels. It is a theory with numerous successful precision predictions, and WRRA is not yet at a stage where it can be said to have replaced it.

## Higgs field and Higgs particle

**Status Established physical concept**

**Plain meaning** The Higgs field is the field through which elementary particles acquire mass terms in the vacuum after electroweak symmetry breaking; the Higgs particle is a quantum excitation of that field.

**In this book** It serves as a representative case for distinguishing an “existence prediction” from “parameter-value calibration.” The Higgs mechanism required the existence of a particle but did not, from theory alone, fix its mass value exactly before experiment.

**Caution** It is an oversimplification to say that the Higgs creates all mass. Most of the mass of protons and neutrons comes from strong-interaction energy.

## Metric

**Status Established geometric and gravitational concept**

**Plain meaning** A rule determining distances and time intervals between events in space and time. In general relativity, the gravitational field is expressed as variation in the spacetime metric itself.

**In this book** It is used to test whether a renderer preserves distance relations when mapping internal states into observable space and time.

**Caution** Obtaining a similar distance table alone does not show that Einstein’s equations are satisfied.

## Euclidean and Lorentzian signatures

**Status Established mathematical-physics concepts**

**Plain meaning** In Euclidean signature all axes are treated as spatial, whereas in Lorentzian signature one time axis has a sign different from the spatial axes. The latter describes causal spacetime.

**In this book** They distinguish whether results from a computationally convenient Euclidean window can extend to a Lorentzian world with real time evolution.

**Caution** Stability of a Euclidean calculation does not by itself guarantee causality or stability in the actual universe.

## Causality and retarded response

**Status Established physical concept**

**Plain meaning** A cause must precede its effect, and a retarded response is a response rule that prevents a future stimulus from changing a past state.

**In this book** It tests whether the common carrier’s linear response has a physically admissible direction of time.

**Caution** The mere presence of a time variable in an equation does not establish causality.

## Scale invariance and primordial perturbations

**Status Established cosmological concepts**

**Plain meaning** Scale invariance is the property that a statistical form remains nearly the same under magnification or reduction. Density perturbations in the early universe seed the cosmic microwave background and galactic structure.

**In this book** It tests whether WRRA transmission conditions can connect to the observed nearly scale-invariant primordial spectrum.

**Caution** Matching a qualitative shape is a different stage from matching observed amplitude, tilt, and non-Gaussianity.

## Planck scale

**Status Scale based on established physical constants**

**Plain meaning** A reference scale of extremely small length and extremely high energy constructed from the speed of light, gravitational constant, and Planck constant. Quantum mechanics and gravity are expected to become simultaneously important there.

**In this book** It is used as an externally supplied physical reference when estimating cosmic information content or minimum resolution.

**Caution** This does not mean that WRRA newly derived Planck values. It uses already known physical constants as inputs.

## Holographic principle

**Status Strong hypothesis and research principle in theoretical physics**

**Plain meaning** The idea that the maximum information in a region may scale with the area of its boundary rather than its volume. It arises from black-hole thermodynamics and quantum-gravity research.

**In this book** It provides background for comparing conventional estimates of the universe’s total information at roughly 10¹²²–10¹²³ bits with the compression question posed by Minimal Computation Cosmology.

**Caution** This number is not an independent WRRA prediction; it belongs to an established family of estimates using the Planck area and the size of the cosmic horizon.

## Entropy and information content

**Status Established physical and informational concepts**

**Plain meaning** Entropy measures the number of possible microstates or the amount of uncertainty. Information content is related to the number of bits required to distinguish and record a state.

**This book distinguishes the cost of storing a complete image of the universe from the compressed cost of storing only LAW, SOURCE, and execution rules.**

**Caution** Thermodynamic entropy, Shannon information, and algorithmic information are related but not identical quantities.

## Renormalization

**Status Established method in quantum field theory and statistical physics**

**Plain meaning** A procedure for organizing how effective parameters vary with the observed energy or length scale, and for consistently connecting divergences in calculations to physical observables.

**In this book** Used when discussing how filters and coupling structures continue across different computational scales.

**Caution** It is inaccurate to describe renormalization as an arbitrary trick that simply erases infinities.

## Superconductivity

Status Established condensed-matter concept + conditional implementation in this book

Plain meaning A collective quantum phenomenon below a critical temperature in which direct-current resistance vanishes, magnetic fields are expelled from the bulk by the Meissner effect, and finite superfluid stiffness and phase order appear together.

In this book It is interpreted not as a property supplied directly by SOURCE, but as a downstream collective phenotype arising when electron pairing, phase locking along relational paths, suppression of phase slips, and electromagnetic boundary response close together.

Caution Reduced resistance alone is insufficient; bulk Meissner fraction, h/(2e) flux quantization, superfluid density, and reproducible critical conditions must also be confirmed. Toy parameters in the 300 K regime do not constitute discovery of an actual room-temperature superconductor.

## Superfluidity

Status Established condensed-matter concept + structural reclassification in this book

Plain meaning A collective quantum phenomenon in which a superfluid component with extremely low viscous loss transports mass according to the phase gradient of a complex order parameter, while circulation is quantized around a closed path.

In this book The helium-4 wetting film is interpreted as converting the vessel boundary into a continuous mass CARRY PATH, with phase relations reconstructed at each present, yielding a downstream collective phenotype.

Caution The Rollin film is not gravitational shielding, and condensate density n₀ generally differs from superfluid mass density ρs. Open-film outflow must also be distinguished from persistent winding current in a closed loop.

## Living closure

Status Operational definition for the book’s external biological application

Plain meaning The condition in which a system, while remaining selectively open to its environment, reconstructs its own reaction network, translation machinery, cell membrane, and reproducible state, and preserves executability after division.

In this book Defined as the intersection of bounded openness, renderer self-reconstruction, and intergenerational executability.

Caution This does not mean that the universe is alive, nor does reproduction of public minimal-cell data validate WRRA cosmology. The 105 min value is a prior calibration value and therefore is not counted as an independent prediction.

## Biochemical executor

Status A term reclassifying established molecular-biological components by WRRA ownership

Plain meaning The material ensemble of polymerases, ribosomes, tRNAs, aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases, translation factors, and energy chemistry that reads DNA or RNA specifications and turns them into actual molecular products.

Caution The presence of an executor’s description in DNA does not cause the first executor to arise spontaneously. Seeded expression must be distinguished from seed-free bootstrap.

## Lineage reproduction number, Rlife

Status WRRA life application of an established branching-process expectation

Plain meaning The average number of executable daughter cells left in the next generation by one mother cell. If P₂ is the probability that both daughters pass and P₁ is the probability that only one passes, then Rlife=P₁+2P₂.

Caution Rlife>1 means supercriticality under the declared probability model; it does not imply permanent survival of a real species or total evolutionary fitness.

## Paired-daughter holdout

Status Independent validation data structure in WRRA Life Model III

Plain meaning Instead of comparing population averages, an unused validation set links the molecular counts, volumes, and subsequent divisions of daughters A and B from the same mother cell within one record.

Caution If symmetric division was already used to construct the model, the same data cannot be counted as an independent predictive success.

## First living lineage

Status Operational life boundary in WRRA Life Model 0

Plain meaning A state in which a bounded chemical system transmits functional differences to descendants and continues to produce, on average, more than one functional daughter lineage.

Caution It is not identical to LUCA, the first protocell, or a modern autonomous cell, and it does not identify the actual first organism in history.

## Environmental renderer

Status Structural role in an origin-of-life scenario

Plain meaning External execution conditions—wet–dry, temperature, photochemical, mineral, and redox cycles—that perform concentration, activation, and division for early compartments.

Caution This concept does not call the environment itself alive or a conscious selector. The selectable unit is a bounded lineage whose internal state changes descendant production.

## Copying-error threshold

Status Exact boundary within the declared branching model

Plain meaning The condition that a longer inherited sequence requires higher copying accuracy at each site for functional descendants to average more than one lineage.

Caution fc=(2K)⁻¹ᐟᴸ holds only under the assumptions of two daughters, mean f, and independent factorization; it is not a universal constant of life.

## Fixed-present neural state

Status External biological application of WRRA’s present-residue interpretation to a conventional state-space representation

Plain meaning A representation that calculates the next neural state from current activity, adaptation, available resources, and current input, without rereading the entire past record.

Caution This does not make Markovianity automatically true. If past paths predict the next state better from the same observed present, the state is insufficient; if no finite extension resolves the problem, the representation fails.

## Computational resource ledger

Status Internal conservation device in the WRRA-Worm toy model

Plain meaning A ledger in which local resource q moves into an environmental reservoir E during activity and returns during recovery, keeping B=Σq+E constant.

Caution It is not a measurement of ATP, heat, or free energy. A real biophysical interpretation requires units and metabolic data to be connected.

## Relation port

Status Hypothetical physical interface in WRRA 2.3

Plain meaning A structure that connects two boundaries far apart in external spacetime as direct neighbors in the fundamental relation network, allowing them to exchange states.

Caution This does not mean that physical space has actually been bent; distance-independent physical coupling and universal matter transport remain OPEN.

## Preferred foliation

Status A physical temporal-order structure studied in relativity and quantum gravity + WRRA’s fixed-present assumption

Plain meaning A structure that orders all events by the same present-layer index k or time function T, preventing a return to a prior present even when spatially nonlocal links exist.

Caution This is the physical cost of giving up exact Lorentz-boost symmetry at the port’s fundamental level; the recovery of Lorentz symmetry under ordinary conditions must be explained.

## Microscopic nonlocality and microcausality

Status Established locality criterion in relativistic QFT and the conflict boundary of WRRA 2.3

Plain meaning In standard local QFT, observables separated by a spacelike interval cannot exert a communicable influence, whereas a genuine superluminal relation port requires a signal outside this condition.

Caution Acyclicity of the fixed present is not equivalent to standard microcausality. WRRA 2.3 replaces it with a different fundamental causal ordering rather than extending it.
