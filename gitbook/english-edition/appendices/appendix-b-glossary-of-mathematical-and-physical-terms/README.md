# Appendix B Glossary of Mathematical and Physical Terms

This appendix is not a dictionary that replaces the equations, but a reading map that helps readers retain the logic of the main text. “Established concept” explains a meaning generally used in textbooks and scholarship; “model concept in this book” explains a usage proposed in the WRRA research program. “Hypothetical connection” is an as-yet unverified interpretation linking those two layers. Equations compress exact relations; this commentary explains in everyday language what those equations are intended to say.
