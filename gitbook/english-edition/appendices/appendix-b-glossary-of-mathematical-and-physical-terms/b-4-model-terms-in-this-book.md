# B-4 Model Terms in This Book

## Minimal Computation Cosmology

**Status Hypothetical perspective of this book**

**Plain meaning The view that the universe generates needed reality using the laws, constants, reading order, and realization conditions of LAW together with a separate minimal SOURCE input, without storing every scene in advance or actualizing every possibility.**

**In this book** It is the overarching philosophy and computational principle that unifies the book.

**Caution** At present it is a research program and hypothesis, not a validated standard cosmology.

## WRRA

**Status Model concept in this book**

**Plain meaning An abbreviation of Wonsik Reality-Renderer Architecture. LAW determines the state space, relations, boundaries, and renderer; a separate SOURCE η opens one history; and the architecture then constructs observable phenotypes from the dynamic state.**

**In this book The current central model that combines LAW’s dimension filter, operations, boundaries, time evolution, and observational instrument with a separate SOURCE boundary input in one execution system.**

**Caution** WRRA is a name proposed in this research and is not yet a standard scholarly term or a fully validated physical theory.

## SOURCE

**Status Model concept in this book**

**Plain meaning A separate boundary input that acts once on the dormant state and opening degrees of freedom prepared by LAW to begin one cosmic history. The current minimal candidate is the momentum displacement η.**

**In this book The object of asking what minimum boundary input, separate from fixed LAW, is required to start one history.**

**Caution Laws, constants, representations, boundaries, and interactions must not be hidden inside SOURCE. The magnitude of η and its physical matching must likewise be tested against external observations.**

## RELATION

**Status Model concept in this book**

**Plain meaning** A rule describing how individual points or values are connected to and influence one another, rather than the points or values alone.

**In this book** It creates a relational network that computes modes and channels connected to a one-dimensional source address in parallel.

## BOUNDARY

**Status Model term extending an established concept**

**Plain meaning** Boundary conditions and decision rules determining which states are permitted, blocked, or able to pass into another layer.

**In this book** They select which possible states can emerge as actual phenotypes.

**Caution** Each version must state clearly whether a boundary is a concrete physical device or a mathematical constraint.

## Actual and phenotype

**Status Model distinction in this book**

**Plain meaning** Actual is an internal execution state larger than the observational output, whereas phenotype is the visible state constructed as an actual record through interaction with an observational apparatus.

**In this book** This distinction allows the Standard Model to be read as a phenotypic grammar and WRRA as a candidate execution structure beneath it.

**Caution** A postulated invisible Actual requires a test condition that produces an observable difference.

## renderer

**Status Model concept in this book**

**Plain meaning** A rule that converts an internal state into a representation of space, time, particles, and measurements that an observer can read. The analogy to a computer graphics card is useful, but the concept also includes physical record formation.

**In this book** It is the core interface of the minimal-computation hypothesis: only the local image needed when observation occurs is output.

**Caution** Renderer does not mean that consciousness freely creates reality; one must define which physical interaction constitutes an output request.

## Dimension filter

**Status Early model concept in this book**

**Plain meaning** A filter that selects, among possible relations and modes, the structures that survive in a particular dimension.

**In this book** Initially an independent model in the research, it is now reinterpreted as a dimensional-selection and representational-transformation module inside WRRA.

**Caution** That dimension itself actually arises because of this filter remains a hypothesis.

## Common carrier

**Status Model concept in this book**

**Plain meaning** The assumption that multiple forces and matter channels are not completely separate from the outset but share one common transmission structure.

**In this book** It connects 15 channels, chiral matter, rank opening, and spectral settlement within one computational framework.

**Caution** This does not mean that a unified gauge field of the Standard Model has been completed.

## Fifteen channels

**Status Model construction in this book**

**Plain meaning** Fifteen internal channels constructed in connection with the chiral fermionic degrees of freedom of one Standard Model generation.

**In this book** They provide the computational arena for testing selective realization and a first-generation inventory capable of anomaly cancellation within the common carrier.

**Caution** Matching the number of channels alone does not derive particle masses, mixing, or the generation structure.

## Spectral locking and settlement

**Status Hypothetical interpretation in this book**

**Plain meaning** The claim that after rank opens, allowed modes do not continue to drift but settle into a stable eigenvalue structure.

**In this book** It seeks to explain how matter, once generated, becomes a sustainable particle-like state.

**Caution** Numerical stability must be distinguished from long-term cosmological stability.

## Selective realization

**Status Model concept in this book**

**Plain meaning** The process of recording only the one result required by a physical query and boundary conditions, rather than turning every possible state simultaneously into observational reality.

**In this book** It is the central idea that maintains both a single universe—ours—and an open future.

Caution The two-stage hypothesis proposes a law-supported set prior to Born weighting, but the realizability factor and single-outcome selector have not yet been derived.

## Law-supported set

Status Hypothetical concept in this book

Plain meaning Before probabilities are assigned, this is the candidate set containing only events allowed by causality, conservation, gauge conditions, interactions, and existing records.

In this book Probability does not create possibilities; it weights possibilities within this set.

Caution A universal method for generating the supported set remains hypothetical and must not be changed ad hoc from experiment to experiment.

## Realizability factor χ\_real and record redundancy R\_rec

Status Hypothetical boundary quantities in this book

Plain meaning χ\_real indicates whether a quantum alternative qualifies to become a visible event, while R\_rec indicates the strength of a record already copied into the environment.

In this book They form a first interface for quantifying the boundary between microscopic openness and macroscopic stability.

Caution The exact dynamics, threshold, and measurement method remain OPEN.

## Open history and single outcome

**Status Hypothetical perspective of this book**

**Plain meaning** The idea that initial values do not prewrite every future event, yet actual records remain one continuous history rather than many branching universes.

**In this book** It allows choice and chance to keep the future open even under fixed laws and constants.

**Caution** An “exact physical law by which only one outcome is realized” remains a gap and requires independent validation.

## Axiom

**Status** Established general concept + axiomatic use in this book

**Plain meaning** A basic proposition provisionally accepted as the starting point of a theory. An axiom does not mean believing an arbitrary assertion; it means exposing the starting points on which subsequent calculation and testing depend.

**In this book** This book freezes eight items specifying what WRRA adopts as basic structure, at a level comparable to the axioms of classical mechanics, relativity, and quantum mechanics.

**Caution** Even if logically consistent conclusions follow from the axioms, whether nature adopts those axioms requires separate observational validation.

## Quantum Refinement

**Status** Established general concept + axiomatic use in this book

**Plain meaning** WRRA’s internal execution layer that assigns quantum amplitudes, phases, interference, and Born probabilities to multiple candidates first permitted by law.

**In this book** It does not discard or weaken quantum mechanics; it connects quantum mechanics to preceding and subsequent layers by specifying the event space in which it is calculated and how it leads to stable records.

**Caution** Saying that “quantum mechanics is inside WRRA” is currently an architectural declaration, not an experimental theorem that predicts every quantum phenomenon more accurately.

## Closure

**Status Research judgment term**

**Plain meaning** The condition in which a particular computational loop closes consistently from beginning to end using only the specified equations, symmetries, and inputs.

**In this book** It marks the achieved scope of subproblems such as electron-gauge closure, the water molecule, and the first-generation inventory.

**Caution** Partial closure does not mean completion, uniqueness, or experimental proof of the entire theory.

## Form factor

Plain meaning A function describing how a charge or magnetic distribution responds as transferred momentum changes when a particle does not appear pointlike. The neutron magnetic form factor is an observational window into the neutron’s internal current structure.

In this book It is used to test whether a common static geometry remains sufficient at finite momentum or whether separate current dressing is required.

## Holdout and freezing

Plain meaning A holdout leaves part of the data unseen while fitting a model and uses it only for final evaluation. Freezing means fixing equations, constants, and judgment rules before evaluation and not refitting them after seeing the result.

Caution An internal holdout that splits one already published dataset is useful, but it is not the same grade as prospectively predicting a new experiment.

## Independent prediction and calibration

**Status Scientific-method distinction**

**Plain meaning** Calibration sets parameters to match already known observations; independent prediction calculates a new value not used at that stage before it is observed.

**In this book** This is the standard WRRA must pass to become a physical theory rather than a re-expression of the Standard Model.

**Caution** There is nothing inherently wrong with using known constants as inputs. What was input and what was newly predicted must, however, be distinguished transparently.

## Information owner

**Status** Status Audit concept in this book

**Plain meaning** The layer that first possesses a piece of information independently, before it is copied or inferred from another layer.

**In this book** It traces where an answer originates among law, SOURCE, renderer, phenotype, present state, and event apparatus.

**Caution** “Owner” means the first computational source of information, not a legal owner.

## Algorithmic information

**Status** Status Established information-theory concept

**Plain meaning** The length of the shortest program that can reproduce specified data to a specified precision. A single generative rule may be far shorter than writing every number explicitly.

**In this book It distinguishes the direct storage of 6,369 modes, the description lengths of LAW and the dormant-state seed, and the description length of SOURCE η.**

**Caution** Because the exact shortest program is generally uncomputable, it must not be identified with direct storage size.

## Topology

**Status** Status Established mathematical and physical concept

**Plain meaning** A global mode of connection that remains unchanged under deformation without tearing or gluing. It distinguishes whether space extends infinitely, is closed, or wraps around multiple times.

**In this book A candidate for LAW’s global structure, or for an explicit boundary datum, that determines whether total cosmic size and quantity are meaningful.**

**Caution** The rotation angle of a complex number is also called “phase” in English and shares a Korean term with topology, but the two concepts are entirely different.

## Spectral measure

**Status** Status Established mathematical concept

**Plain meaning** A distribution rule specifying how much weight a state assigns to different eigenvalues or energy intervals.

**In this book** It represents not only a list of discrete modes but also continuous spectra and the weights of particle responses.

**Caution** A spectrum’s locations and the weights at those locations are different information.

## Pole and threshold

**Status** Status Established quantum-field-theory concepts

**Plain meaning** A pole of a propagator represents a sharp response like a stable particle, whereas a threshold is the energy at which a continuum allowing multiple excitations begins.

**In this book** They determine how mass appears as an observational response in an executed propagator, rather than as a mere input table.

**Caution** Not every pole is an eternally stable particle; resonances, branch cuts, and renormalization conditions must also be considered.

## Minisuperspace

A reduced cosmological state space retaining only a few degrees of freedom, such as the FLRW scale factor a(t) and homogeneous fields, instead of the infinitely many degrees of freedom of general spacetime. An exact solution here is not the same as an exact solution in the full four-dimensional theory.

## Hamiltonian constraint

The energy–gravity constraint that admissible states must satisfy in the canonical formulation of general relativity. An impulse that changes only momentum is not physical initial data if it leaves this constraint surface.

## Bogoliubov coefficients and quench

In a time-dependent background, Bogoliubov coefficients describe how strongly earlier vacuum modes mix with later particle modes. A quench is a procedure that changes a parameter rapidly. Because an instantaneous change can create high-energy divergences, a smooth profile is important.

## Fock state

A representation of quantum-field states with particle numbers 0, 1, 2, and so on. Opening the support of a one-particle representation is different from actually producing a gauge-neutral particle–antiparticle pair.
