# Chapter 42 The Perspective Left by Minimal Computation Cosmology

The core of Minimal Computation Cosmology is not a claim that the universe resembles a human computer. It is an operational intuition that asks, within one ledger, about the size of source data required to realize a universe, the initial read order, autonomous temporal evolution, selective realization, and conditions of shared records. Common structures are reused instead of recreating the same relations repeatedly, and observable differences are implemented only at required boundaries.

The distinction between primes and composites began in version 1.0 as an arithmetic analogy explaining minimal generation and reuse of relations. Version 2.0 promotes it to a MODEL AXIOM of the prime-port registry. Primes, however, are independent arithmetic addresses—not physical particles, charges, masses, or frequencies in hertz—while composites are relation addresses preserving valuation incidence. Promotion to a physical quantity is permitted only after R\_phys.

The dimensional filter placed a selection layer between possibility and reality. Not every possible degree of freedom is observed in the same way; a subset of relations passing locality, stability, information conservation, and boundary recording appears as one reality.

This perspective raised the question of value ownership. Unless one distinguishes whether mass and charge arise from LAW's representation and coupling rules, from transport and boundaries, or only in the observer's chosen units, the meaning of a calculation becomes obscure.

Minimal computation is not a proven law that nature must choose the shortest program. At present, it is a heuristic for comparing implementation candidates and a design principle asking whether they reduce redundancy while preserving necessary information and physical diversity.

Nevertheless, this perspective unified the research as a whole. The arithmetic filter, common carrier, WRRA, and finite Jacobi runtime are different expressions of one question: how does observable reality emerge from more possibilities through fewer common structures?

_possibility → filter → shared transport → observable reality_ (6.2)

If C(ρ) = {Ω : R(Ω) = ρ} is the set of Actual states compatible with the same Reality state ρ, differences absent from Reality remain as multiplicity and correlation structure in this compatibility fiber. Rendering deficit is therefore not an amount of destroyed information, but the structure of distinctions not yet resolved in Reality.

The information and thermodynamic ledgers must state who owns hidden information and which algebra can access it.

The recovery condition is revised accordingly. The objective is not to invert every Actual microstate, but to reproduce the intended lawful evolution E\_lawful on the protected physical algebra A\_protected, within a specified tolerance and time interval.

D ∘ R ≃ E\_lawful on A\_protected
