# Chapter 43 A Map for the Next Researcher

A researcher continuing this work need not initially read every paper with equal weight. It is best to begin with WRRA's definitions of LAW–SOURCE–RENDERER–PHENOTYPE and the claim ledger, then examine path equivalence, recoverability, and time-uniform conditions.

Microscopic implementation can be tested beginning with the finite Jacobi benchmark. First reproduce the spectral density, recurrence, visible port, and computational window exactly; then vary order N, α, threshold, and Euclidean window to determine how convergence and recurrence change.

The first attack on factor coupling already left two branches. A simple off-diagonal coupling failed with an order-dependent positivity corridor, while a gauge-covariant derivative coupling passed finite-order positivity but produced carrier-dependent characteristic cones. The next boss is continuum convergence and microcausality of a lower-order covariant coupling with a fixed common principal cone.

In particle physics, minimal calibration values such as the electron mass and α must be frozen, and one mass ratio or mixing combination selected as held-out. Moving Standard-Model parameters into different notation must be separated from predicting a new value.

In cosmology, autonomous opening is not immediately identified with the Big Bang. Background expansion, the primordial spectrum, reheating, long-time recovery resources, and backreaction are calculated in sequence, then tested against sealed data such as the age and expansion rate of the universe.

In every direction, the result must preserve the starting assumptions, computational code, inputs used, failed branches, and version DOI together. The next researcher needs not only a conclusion, but exact coordinates from which the work can be restarted.
